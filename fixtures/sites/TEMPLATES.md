# Third-party templates

- `startbootstrap-agency`: [Start Bootstrap Agency](https://github.com/StartBootstrap/startbootstrap-agency), MIT, copied from the repository as GitHub's "Download ZIP" would give it (Bootstrap 5, source in `src/`, built site in `dist/`).
- `landwind`: [Landwind](https://github.com/themesberg/landwind) by Themesberg, MIT (Tailwind CSS and Flowbite, compiled `output.css` beside its source files).

Their licenses are in each folder. The other sites in this folder were written for Rótulo's tests.

# Furnace Hill Coffee

`furnace-hill` is a realistic demo blog. It is the journal of a made-up two-person coffee roastery in Sheffield, with seven static pages and no JavaScript. Its people, prices and address are fiction. The type is Source Serif 4 by Adobe, OFL 1.1, from [adobe-fonts/source-serif](https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-serif). The photographs come from Wikimedia Commons:
