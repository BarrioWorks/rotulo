Our story

A bakery that grew one loaf at a time

Fifteen years, one stubborn sourdough starter and a lot of very early mornings.

2009

Forty loaves and a trestle table

Elsie Moreau came back to Port Ellery after ten years baking in Lyon and Montreal. She rented the old net loft on Wharf Lane, bought a second-hand deck oven, and started selling bread from a table by the door.

The first winter she sold out by nine every morning. By spring there was a queue, and by summer there was a coffee machine and a second baker.

What we believe

Good bread is slow, local and shared

Take the time

Long fermentation makes bread that tastes better, keeps longer and is easier to digest. We will not rush it.

Buy close to home

Flour from New England mills, milk from Halloway Farm, fruit from orchards within an hour of the harbour.

Nothing goes to waste

Unsold bread goes to the food pantry every evening. Stale ends become our bread pudding and croutons.
