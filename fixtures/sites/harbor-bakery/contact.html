Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

Do you have gluten-free options?

We bake a gluten-free almond cake and buckwheat cookies daily, but our kitchen handles a lot of wheat flour, so we cannot promise they are free of traces.

Can I order a celebration cake?

Yes. We need five days’ notice for cakes. Send us a message with the date, the number of guests and any flavours you love.

Are dogs welcome?

Very. There is a water bowl by the door and a jar of biscuits behind the counter.
