## Scope and verdicts

Reviewed on 20 October 2026. Read launch decisions 1 to 5 and audit section 4 before tracing implementation. Production defaults are the limit baseline. No page changes are applied.

TRUE means implementation or an owner decision supports the claim. PARTLY means the core works but the condition, scope or unit needs correction. UNSUPPORTED means the required evidence does not establish the claim.

Each row quotes a visible factual passage. Related assertions in one passage share a verdict. Repeated passages are separate rows when their page or wording differs. Navigation and action labels repeat features covered below. Pure persuasion, advice, illustrative file trees and image alternative text are not separate rows. Limit rows join the exact term and value with a colon. Nonbreaking spaces are normalised.

Decision 9 requires real editor screenshots of the Furnace Hill sample. It supports the documented provenance requirement, not independent verification of a capture date or exact pixels. The audit also records the real sample journey. Claims about third-party export frequency have no implementation evidence.

References are repository-relative file and line anchors. They point to implementations rather than the copy under review. Decision 5 authorises the free-to-try and no-billing default. Absence claims use the complete ownership or auth flow as evidence.
