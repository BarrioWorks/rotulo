2. How it works

  1. 1

    Import

    Drop the ZIP. Pagehaven checks it and lists anything it left out.

  2. 2

    Change it on the page

    Click and type. Swap a photo. Fix a link. Every change saves as a draft.

  3. 3

    Publish

    Review what changed, then go live. Earlier versions stay in history.

What it handles

Exported static sites. Words, pictures, links and supported styles.

What it refuses

Source projects that still need a build step, such as an unbuilt React or Vite folder. It says so and tells you what to export.

What stays yours

Your draft stays private until you publish. You can roll back to an earlier version.
